**Effective Date:** [Date]

This privacy policy applies to the FlowShift app (hereby referred to as "Application") for mobile devices that was created by [Developer Name] (hereby referred to as "Service Provider") as an Ad-Supported / Free / Freemium / Commercial service. This service is intended for use "AS IS".

## Information Collection and Use

The Application is designed to operate completely locally on your device. To provide its core functionality—helping you manage your app usage and complete physical exercises—the Application requests access to specific device features and data:

*   **App Usage Data:** The Application requires access to your device's usage statistics (`PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS`) to monitor how much time you spend on different apps. This data is used solely to enforce the app limits you set.
*   **Camera:** The Application requires access to your device's camera (`CAMERA`) to track and verify your movements using on-device machine learning (MediaPipe) during exercise routines. The camera frames are processed locally in real-time and are **never** recorded, stored, or transmitted.
*   **Draw Over Other Apps:** The Application requires permission to display over other apps (`SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW`) in order to block access to restricted apps when your set time limits are reached.

### Local Data Processing

All data collected and processed by the Application—including your app usage statistics, camera feed for exercise verification, and personal settings—remains **strictly on your device**. 
The Application does **not** have internet access permission (`android.permission.INTERNET`). Therefore, no data is ever transmitted, uploaded to the cloud, or shared with the Service Provider or any third parties.

## Third-Party Access

Since the Application does not transmit data over the internet, no third parties have access to your usage data, camera feed, or personal settings. 

## Children

The Service Provider does not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. Since the Application operates entirely offline and does not collect personal data, it is safe for use by all age groups.

## Security

The Service Provider values your trust in providing your information. However, since the Application operates entirely offline and data is stored locally on your device using standard Android security mechanisms, the security of your data is primarily dependent on the security of your physical device and its operating system.
